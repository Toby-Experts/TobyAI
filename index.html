Asked: Payroll tax for the year ending 30 June 2026, with staff in Queensland, New South Wales and Victoria
Computed answer

Payroll tax of $338,410.23 is payable across the three jurisdictions on $7,450,000 of Australian taxable wages.

Each state's threshold is apportioned to its share of Australia-wide wages, which is why two of the three thresholds fall well below their headline figure.

Where your data sits

Five commitments early access ships on, each of them a design decision rather than a policy promise.

No PII to AI

Names, tax file numbers, bank details and salaries never reach an AI provider. Identifiers are hashed at the boundary.

Australian residency

Your compliance data is held and processed in Australia, in Sydney. It is not shipped offshore for convenience, so it is not a cross-border disclosure you have to paper over.

Enforced multi-factor

Sign-in requires a second factor, and access to a client's data is scoped to the people you authorise.

No training on your data

Toby trains no model on your data. The models it calls run on Amazon Bedrock, whose terms say your content is not used to improve the base models and is not shared with any model provider.

Auditable by design

Each answer records the schedule version, the source it came from and a fingerprint of the assessment, so a file note can be reconstructed later.
