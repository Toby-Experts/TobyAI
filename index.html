What a firm actually asks, in plain language

Across the group

Four entities employ in Queensland, New South Wales and Victoria. What payroll tax does each state levy on the group's wages, and who lodges?

Restructures and exits

A redundancy round covers 14 roles, service between 2 and 19 years. How much of each payment is tax free, and what is withheld on the balance?

Construction, on site

We have carpenters and concreters on a commercial site in Brisbane. Which classification covers a CW3 concreter, what is the ordinary hourly rate once the industry allowance is in, and where does the award say so?

Prior years, on the law of the day

We are amending the year ended 30 June 2024. What was the New South Wales rate and threshold then, and which compilation says so?

Each is answered from an attested schedule or from the instrument, with the figure computed in code, the working shown and the provision cited. Some questions Toby refuses. Employee or contractor, whether related entities are grouped, and whether a position is reasonably arguable are judgements a person records, with evidence, before a figure is computed.

Why a firm can rely on it

Six commitments, each a design decision rather than a policy promise.

Computed, not generated

Every figure is computed in versioned Python from an attested schedule. The engine imports no AI, so no model writes a number.

No PII to AI

Names, tax file numbers, bank details and salaries never reach an AI provider. Identifiers are hashed at the boundary.

No training on your data

Toby trains no model on your data. Its models run on Amazon Bedrock, whose terms say your content is not used to improve the base models and is not shared with any provider.

Australian residency

Your compliance data is held and processed in Sydney, so there is no cross-border disclosure to paper over.

Enforced multi-factor

Sign-in runs on Auth0 in its Australian region and requires a second factor. Access to a client is scoped to the people you authorise.

Auditable by design

Each answer records the schedule version, the source and a fingerprint of the assessment, so a file note can be reconstructed later.
