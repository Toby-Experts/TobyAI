What TobyAI covers

GST and BAS

The GST Act and the BAS labels, read together so an unmapped account lands on the right label for a registered agent to confirm.

What you get

A proposed BAS label for each unmapped bookkeeping account, grounded in the label definitions rather than a guess, for a human to apply.

GST registration, tax invoice and attribution questions answered from the Act and the ATO's own guidance, with the paragraph cited.

A Xero data health check that finds the GST codings a BAS will trip on before the BAS is prepared.

Sources
A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) Act 1999 and Regulations 2019
Federal Register of Legislation, with the transition and imposition Acts.
GST rulings and determinations
Retrieved from ato.gov.au, dated as printed.
BAS label definitions
ATO business activity statement guidance.
How you reach it

BAS remediation in the portal: proposals from a de-identified account summary, applied by a human or registered agent.

Xero data health check, free, from a connected organisation or an uploaded export.

Corpus research over the GST Act and rulings.

How every figure is made

Computed in auditable Python from a versioned schedule that carries its source, its retrieval date and who attested it. No AI model originates a rate, a threshold, a date or a legal position.

Your data stays in Sydney. No name, tax file number, bank detail or salary reaches an AI provider, and nothing you send is used to train a model.

Status

Research and the health check are available in limited access. BAS remediation proposes; it never lodges, and TobyAI never writes to your ledger.
