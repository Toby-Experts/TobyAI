What TobyAI covers

Fringe benefits tax

FBT liability from the rate and gross-up factors the ATO publishes, with the benefit categories cited to the Assessment Act.

What you get

Taxable value grossed up at the type 1 or type 2 factor and taxed at the FBT rate, each figure from the attested schedule for the year.

Whether something is a fringe benefit is a judgement: TobyAI surfaces the test and its citation, and a human records the decision before anything is computed.

How every figure is made

Computed in auditable Python from a versioned schedule that carries its source, its retrieval date and who attested it. No AI model originates a rate, a threshold, a date or a legal position.

Your data stays in Sydney. No name, tax file number, bank detail or salary reaches an AI provider, and nothing you send is used to train a model.
